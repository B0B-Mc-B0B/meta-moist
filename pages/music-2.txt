<p><strong>Music 2</strong> is a channel in [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums]] originally intended to be used for commands controlling the secondary music bot BoomBoxBunny. BoomBoxBunny was eventually replaced by the custom music bot Kitten DJs. After Kitten DJs went offline, the channel received very little activity, but was not archived. In January of 2026, Sid sent a skull emoji. This was followed by several other users responding with skull emojis, and eventually skull-related images and GIFs. This led to the channel becoming much more active, and primarily dedicated to skullposting.</p>
