<p>RNGdle is a game where the player makes the game generate a random number between 0 and 1 million. This number is then awarded badges based on the properties of the number. A number is then given a "rarity" based on the amount of Experience Points it gained from its badges. Although an accurate rarity ranking would give all numbers an equal score, as each one is equally likely to be generated, the RNGdle ranking system is biased towards certain numbers due to the nature of the badges.</p>
