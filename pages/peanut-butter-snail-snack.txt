[img[images/misc/peanut-butter-snail-snack.png|The peanut butter snail snack image posted by onceiatefood]img]<p>The peanut butter snail snack is a snack shaped like a snail typically constructed from celery, peanut butter, chocolate chips, pretzels, and an apple slice. In the [[meta-moist|Meta Moist canon]], it is capable of ruining your life. The snail depicted by the snack unusally has eyes on the head rather than on the ends of the eyestalks.</p><p>The original uncropped image also includes a butterfly snack, although its origin is unknown as it has been posted countless times on several websites by people evidently looking for snack clout. The peanut butter snail snack was posted in [[moist|Moist]] by onceiatefood, with the caption "How a Peanut Butter Snail Snack Can Ruin Your Life". This is a screenshot of a thumbnail that links to a 2015 article by Jen Warman, using the snail snack as an example of how depictions of high-effort parenting endeavours on social media could make a parent feel inadequate to the point of spanking their kids. The informality and extremity of this article was criticised in several Pinterest reposts.</p><p>The peanut butter snail snack picture was recreated on [[Wplace]] in the Olsky District DashNet zone by [[Fractyl]] and [[Fry]] during the late March and early April of 2026.
<h2>Recipe</h2>
[img[images/misc/fry-snail-snack.png|A peanut butter snail snack made by [[Fry]], with some deviations from the source]img]
<p>
Ingredients:
<ul>
<li>a rib of celery</li>
<li>peanut butter</li>
<li>a mini hard pretzel</li>
<li>two small chocolate chips</li>
<li>an apple</li>
</ul>
Steps:
<ol>
<li>Cut a stick from the rib of celery about 2 inches in length.</li>
<li>Apply a glob of peanut butter to the celery such that it spans the length.</li>
<li>Take a slice from an apple approximately 1 centimeter thick, with parallel cuts to prevent the snail from becoming top-heavy.</li>
<li>Stick the apple slice in the peanut butter, closer to one end of the celery stick to leave room on the other end for the snail's head.</li>
<li>On the exposed peanut butter, place the chocolate chips to represent the [[snail-eyes|snail's eyes]].</li>
<li>Break off two small curved sections of the hard pretzel and place them beside the chips to mimic the eyestalks.</li>
<li>Apply thin lines of peanut butter to the exposed flesh of the apple, following the curve of the apple's skin. Best done with a piping bag, which you can make by just cutting the corner off a resealable plastic bag.</li>
</ol>
This is a lot of effort for one snail snack, so if you're actually interested in this snack maybe do six at once. You'll need six times as much peanut butter, celery, and chocolate chips. You could probably still use just one apple, depending on how good your apple slicing skills are.
</p>
