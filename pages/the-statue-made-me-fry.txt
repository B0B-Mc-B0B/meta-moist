<p><strong>The statue made me fry</strong> is a line from the song <cite>The Statue Got Me High</cite> by They Might Be Giants. In [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums]], the phrase is more often used to refer to one of several [[meta-moist|Meta Moist]] videos that use the song.</p>
<h2>Videos</h2>
[img[images/misc/the-statue-made-me-fry.jpg|A frame from the original The statue made me Fry animation]img]
<p>On 22/03/2026 at 12:15 am, Fractyl posted an animation to [[lobby|Lobby]] depicting a statue (represented by the icon for the New world order achievement) turning [[fractyl|Fractyl]] into [[fry|Fry]] while other server members watched. The line "The statue made me fry" from <cite>The Statue Got Me High</cite> plays in the background. The animation ends with the "fry from the meta moist discord server" GIF. On 19/09/2026 at 11:53 pm, Fractyl posted a video to [[meta-moist-thread|Meta Moist]] titled "the statue made me fry (full version)". The video has Fractyl singing DashNet related lyrics over a full instrumental of <cite>The Statue Got Me High</cite>. The song is accompanied by various images and text that are sometimes related to the lyric being sung.</p>
